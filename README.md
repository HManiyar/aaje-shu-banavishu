# Aaje Shu Banavishu? · આજે શું બનાવીશું?

**Your Daily Food Inspiration** — a simple Jain-friendly evening food planner.

Pick the vegetables and ingredients you have at home (type them in Gujarati, English, or Gujarati in English letters), tap **Suggest What to Cook**, and get practical evening snack and light-meal ideas that follow your family's food rules.

**Open the app:** https://hmaniyar.github.io/aaje-shu-banavishu/

- Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript — no server, no sign-up, works offline once loaded.
- Food rules (no onion/garlic, no root vegetables, etc.) are set in Settings.
- Favourites, your own recipes and photos are saved only in the browser on that device.
